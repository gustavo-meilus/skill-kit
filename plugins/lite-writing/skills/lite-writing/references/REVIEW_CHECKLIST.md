# Lite Writing Review Checklist

Check the finished prose in this order.

## Content

- The output answers the actual task.
- Every necessary fact, caveat, constraint, and example remains.
- Technical names, code, commands, exact errors, numbers, units, negation, and scope words remain exact.
- Genuine uncertainty remains explicit.
- Required order and dependencies remain explicit.

## Compression

- Greetings, pleasantries, stock introductions, and process narration are removed unless useful.
- Filler adverbs and empty hedges are removed.
- Repeated explanations and repeated conclusions are stated once.
- Long phrases are shortened only when precision is preserved.
- Headings, bullets, and transitions exist only when they clarify real structure.

## Grammar and clarity

- Articles and ordinary grammar remain intact.
- Sentences are complete unless an intentional fragment is clearly appropriate to the medium.
- No cryptic abbreviations or broken syntax were invented for terseness.
- Security-sensitive, irreversible, or ordered procedures are explicit enough to prevent misreading.

## Scope

- Narrative, literary, voice-controlled, quoted, or style-exemplar passages were not normalized into Lite prose.
- Mixed documents apply Lite mode only to eligible explanatory material.
