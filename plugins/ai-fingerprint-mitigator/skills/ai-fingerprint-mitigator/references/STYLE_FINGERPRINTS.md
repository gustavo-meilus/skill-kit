# Formulaic Style Fingerprints

These are editing signals, not authorship evidence. Diagnose patterns across the passage rather than banning words mechanically.

## Canned framing

Signals:

- social or generic preambles before the substance;
- "this article will" or "let us explore" framing that adds no value;
- generic conclusions that repeat the introduction;
- assistant narration about what it is doing.

Revision: begin with the actual claim, action, or context the reader needs.

## Repetitive architecture

Signals:

- every paragraph uses claim -> explanation -> summary;
- consecutive sentences begin the same way;
- many paragraphs have nearly identical length;
- repeated three-part lists or forced symmetry without semantic need;
- repeated "not X, but Y" or question-answer templates.

Revision: organize by the material's real relationships. Vary shape only when the content requires it.

## Over-structured presentation

Signals:

- headings for trivial distinctions;
- bullets for information that reads more clearly as one paragraph;
- repeated bold labels inside already clear sections;
- a summary after each small section plus another final summary.

Revision: use the least structure that improves comprehension.

## Generic abstraction

Signals:

- vague nouns such as "journey", "landscape", "ecosystem", or "dynamic" where a concrete noun is available;
- weak verbs hidden behind nominalizations;
- generic intensifiers and uplift adjectives;
- broad claims without specific evidence or actors.

Revision: use specific nouns, precise verbs, and concrete evidence already supported by the source.

## Transition saturation

Signals:

- nearly every paragraph begins with a connector;
- transitions state obvious relationships;
- decorative connectors replace actual logic.

Revision: omit the transition when adjacency already makes the relationship clear. Keep transitions that encode cause, contrast, sequence, or exception.

## Voice flattening

Signals:

- the rewrite sounds polished but no longer sounds like the author;
- contractions, humor, directness, technical density, or paragraph rhythm disappear;
- unusual but intentional diction is replaced with generic professional wording.

Revision: preserve stable author traits unless the user asked to change them.

## Artificial humanization

Signals:

- inserted typos or random fragments;
- invented anecdotes, opinions, slang, or emotional reactions;
- arbitrary sentence variation that creates noise;
- intentional inconsistency or factual looseness.

Revision: remove these tactics. Natural prose comes from accurate specificity, meaningful rhythm, and fit to audience and purpose.
