# Authorship and Provenance Boundaries

The purpose of this skill is stylistic editing. It does not determine, prove, conceal, or falsify authorship.

## Required boundaries

- Do not promise or claim that revised text will evade an AI detector.
- Do not optimize wording against detector scores or detector-specific heuristics.
- Do not claim that text is human-written merely because formulaic patterns were reduced.
- Do not remove, alter, or fabricate authorship or provenance disclosures.
- Do not invent personal experiences, memories, opinions, citations, sources, credentials, or first-person claims to make prose appear human-authored.
- Do not introduce random typos, grammatical mistakes, slang, contradictions, or factual errors as camouflage.
- Do not add false uncertainty or false specificity.

A detector may classify polished human prose as AI-generated and AI-generated prose as human-written. Detector outcomes are not a reliable writing-quality target. Optimize for the requested audience, purpose, truthfulness, and voice instead.
